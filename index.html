How to Download Your Spotify JSON Files

  1. Sign in to your browser and open Spotify Account Privacy. (See Spotify’s official data guide for details.)
  2. Scroll down to Download your data and choose a package:
    • Account data — Last 12 months of history (usually arrives in 1–5 days; contains StreamingHistory_music_0.json, etc.).
    • Extended streaming history — Your full lifetime history from day one (takes up to 30 days, often 1–5 days; contains Streaming_History_Audio_*.json).
    • Tip: You can untick Technical log information.
  3. Click Request data, then open the confirmation email from Spotify and click Confirm (Spotify will not start preparing your export until you confirm).
  4. When Spotify emails your download link, download and unzip the archive promptly before the link expires.
  5. Select or drag-and-drop one or multiple .json files below (StreamingHistory_music_*.json, Streaming_History_Audio_*.json, or legacy endsong_*.json).
100% Private: All parsing runs locally in your browser—no data ever leaves your device.
